The performance

A cockroach becomes an unlikely muse in a one-woman operetta that moves from intimacy to outer space and back again.

The Cockroach Diary is Nicoletta Todesco’s song cycle for voice, classical guitar and electric guitar. Through theatrical interludes and surreal conversations with an uninvited houseguest, the work explores vulnerability, resilience and artistic identity through contemporary classical music, storytelling, noise and humor.

For this National Sawdust performance, the piece is reimagined as an immersive audiovisual experience with visuals by new media artist and filmmaker Xuan, transforming the stage into a dreamlike environment.

Nicoletta Todesco

Voice, guitar, story.

Nicoletta Todesco is a Brooklyn-based guitarist and composer whose performances move between classical and electric guitar, original music and storytelling. Her debut album The Cockroach Diary was released in 2025. She studied at the Yale School of Music and is the director of the New York City Guitar Orchestra.
